The fee is 0.3% of what a rescue moves, at least 0.0001 ETH, plus 0.00001 ETH a day while a loan is watched. On this $8 test loan the floor applies (about 14% of the repayment); on a $100,000 loan, a $22,857 repayment costs about $69, against about $2,500 lost to a liquidation.

Rebound's rescue loop, from price event to callback

One rescue makes a round trip between two chains. Reactive Network is what lets the watcher react to Base's price events and call back into Base, with no server or keeper bot.

  1. 1. Price update on Base. A Chainlink-style feed emits AnswerUpdated. 0xcc5e…914a
  2. 2. Lasna checks the trigger. ShieldReactive re-prices the loans near the new price and asks for a rescue for each one under its trigger, or about to be. 0xcb9a…c72a
  3. 3. The Base shield re-checks the live loan before it spends anything, then repays USDC or supplies WETH by the smallest amount that reaches the target. 0x7686…6933
  4. 4. The updated position goes back to Lasna in the same transaction's PositionSnapshot, so the watcher's copy is current again.

Separately, a periodic sweep on Reactive's Cron10000, about every 3 hours, re-checks loans a price update missed and refreshes day-old copies. Rescues take seconds because they come from price updates, not from the sweep.

    Rebound against repaying upfront

    We replayed real Chainlink ETH/USD updates from four crashes (May 2021, August 2024, October 2025, February 2026) against identical loans with the same reserve, 20% of the debt, under three strategies.

    In these scenarios Rebound matched upfront repayment's liquidation count, cost $268 more in total, and left $17,724 of the reserves unspent. It was never late, even with a 300 s delay.

    Rebound's fees pay its callback gas

    Every fee is unwrapped to ETH inside the shield, and the callback proxy takes its gas from that balance. A small daily watch fee covers loans that are watched but never rescued. Execution on Lasna is paid in lREACT, funded by hand on testnet; the panel shows how much work it does.

    Everything runs on Base Sepolia and Reactive Lasna testnets with test tokens. The pool copies Aave V3's function names and health factor maths so the demo can move the price.
