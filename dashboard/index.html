Parapet's rescue loop, from price event to callback

The oracle on Base Sepolia emits a price update. ShieldReactive on Reactive Lasna re-prices every mirrored loan and requests a callback for each one under its trigger.

The shield on Base checks the live loan again, supplies the smallest top-up that restores the target, and turns its fee into the ETH that pays the callback.

Parapet's rescue latency, measured on testnets

Each round resets the loan, drops WETH from $2,000 to $1,800 and counts Base blocks from the price event to the rescue. Base Sepolia makes a block every 200 ms.

Parapet's fees pay its callback gas

Every fee is unwrapped to ETH inside the shield, and the callback proxy takes its gas from that balance. Execution on Lasna, about 0.04 lREACT a rescue, is still funded by hand.

Everything runs on Base Sepolia and Reactive Lasna testnets with test tokens. The pool copies Aave V3's function names and health factor maths so the demo can move the price.
