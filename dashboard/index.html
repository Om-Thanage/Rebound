Aave gives liquidators a 5% bonus on WETH collateral. Rebound keeps 0.3% of the value it moves, with a floor of 0.0001 ETH, gas included.

Rebound's rescue loop, from price event to callback

A Chainlink-style feed on Base Sepolia emits AnswerUpdated. ShieldReactive on Reactive Lasna re-prices only the loans whose trigger price is near the new price, and requests a callback for each one under its trigger, or about to be if the price keeps falling at the same speed.

The shield on Base checks the live loan again, repays debt or supplies collateral by the smallest amount that restores the target, and turns its fee into the ETH that pays the callback.

The same contract also listens to the feed on Ethereum Sepolia, which can move first, follows the feed if Chainlink swaps its aggregator, and wakes on Reactive's CRON to catch anything a tick missed.

Rebound's rescue latency, measured on testnets

Each round resets the loan, drops WETH from $2,000 to $1,800 and counts Base blocks from the price event to the rescue. Base Sepolia makes a block every 200 ms.

    Rebound's fees pay its callback gas

    Every fee is unwrapped to ETH inside the shield, and the callback proxy takes its gas from that balance. A small daily watch fee covers loans that are watched but never rescued. Execution on Lasna is paid in lREACT, funded by hand on testnet; the panel shows how much work it does.

    Everything runs on Base Sepolia and Reactive Lasna testnets with test tokens. The pool copies Aave V3's function names and health factor maths so the demo can move the price.
